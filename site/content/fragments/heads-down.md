In a restaurant, a metro and an airport gate, almost everyone these days sits with their head down and their face lit blue by a phone. Families share a table and strangers share a bench, and every person is looking at a different screen.

The first iPhone arrived less than twenty years ago, and since then a small rectangular object that we call a mobile phone has come to sit in almost every hand in places like these, changing the way we interact with each other.
